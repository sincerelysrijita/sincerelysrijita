A place to put my thoughts.

A beginning, perhaps.

I used to write everyday at one point of time. writing for me was therapeutic and it provided a comfort that talking to real people didn't. when I wrote within the confines of my own mind and paper and pen, I was free. i was free to speak what I wanted and no one could judge me for it.

it wasn't until I became a teenager, a rebellious one infact, that I stopped writing. I stopped keeping a diary which accounted for all the activities I did. my parents had no sense of security. no sense of privacy.

the very reason why I started writing (to speak without being seen) was violated and I stopped writing.

now my teen years are coming to an end. two more years, and then I'll be a legally called adult. im not ready to become one yet. which is why I want to document whatever mischief I make. the very things I do which disappoint my parents.

"cause I'm just a teenage dirtbag, baby"

A little something to remember this day by.
