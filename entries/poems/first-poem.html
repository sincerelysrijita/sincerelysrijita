Poem's I Wouldn't Write

I wish i could write you a poem But i think i have ran out of words To say how i feel And I think i ran out of ink So i guess i’ll just bleed And i’d bleed, i’d bleed, i’d bleed I’d fill the pages with crimson And colour them red And i would smudge it all off Then go and lie on my bed I would tell you a million things in my head, Never to your face And i’d tell you how much i hate you And that you’re the only reason why I keep on waking up every day. And i’d look for you everywhere In the road, in the crowds, and In the eyes of the beautiful stranger I saw And I'll see your fingers intertwined in my hands And i’ll go back to the day you told me you loved me Run a hundred other universes in my head the one where you didn't leave, the one where you stayed. But then I'll go write you a poem Even though i said i could not (i lied) And I'll carve out the words carefully Just to let you know i tried And you’ll know how much i love you, You’ll see it in between the words i write In the spaces, in the commas, and Every other thing in sight But then like you, I will leave Too sudden, too soon, too unexpected And i’d be untraceable, Was it unprecedented? However I will write you a poem Close the windows and accept my fate, Then eventually i’ll walk out the door Don't worry, In the end I'll be someone we both can hate.
