A PAGE FROM MY JOURNAL · NO. 001

A place to put
my thoughts.

17 November 2026 · 3 min read

On beginnings, remembering, and making a little space for the things that matter.

Perhaps every beginning is a little ordinary. There is no grand announcement, no particular occasion. Sometimes, all it takes is a quiet afternoon and the decision to finally begin.

I've always liked the idea of keeping a diary. Somewhere to put all the thoughts that don't necessarily belong in a conversation. The observations, the memories, the little things that might seem insignificant today but could mean something years from now.

So this is my attempt at creating that space. A small corner of the internet where I can write without having to worry about whether every sentence is perfect.

"Some things are worth remembering, even when they seem ordinary."

I don't know what this archive will eventually become. Perhaps it will hold poems, photographs, stories about people I've met, places I've been, and versions of myself I haven't met yet.

For now, it's enough that I've started.

Until next time,
Srijita.
